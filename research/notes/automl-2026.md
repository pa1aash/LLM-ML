summary: 'AutoML Conference 2026 homepage (Sep 28-Oct 1, 2026, University of Ljubljana,
  Slovenia). Confirms the conference has an ''ABCD'' paper track (Call for Papers
  with ''Details on ABCD Track''), a separate ''Call for Hot-off-the-Press Track,''
  a ''Call for Late-Breaking Abstracts,'' and a ''Call for Tutorials and Workshops.''
  Program already lists tutorials including ''From AutoML to AgenticML: LLMs as the
  New SearchOperator'' and ''Hardware-Aware AutoML for Edge AI,'' confirming LLM-guided
  search is an active topic area for this venue. General/PC chairs: Tome Eftimov (General
  Chair), Atlas Wang, Elena Raponi, Lars Kotthoff (PC Chairs). The homepage itself
  does NOT state page limits, anonymity policy, or any stance on negative/null results
  — those live on the dedicated /call-for-papers/ page, fetched separately as a Phase
  2 lead.'
