## Per-workshop detail

### AI for Meta-Science
- **Scope:** research production has outpaced quality control — peer review, reproducibility norms, publication criteria, incentive structures, funding allocation; scaling evaluation alongside production.
- **Page limits / exclusions:** 4 pages (concise technical) or 8 pages (position). Excludes references; unlimited appendix.
- **Template / footnote:** NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX template, footnote changed to "Submitted to AI for Meta-Science workshop (NeurIPS 2026)."
- **Checklist:** not required.
- **Anonymity:** NOT STATED
- **Archival:** NOT STATED
- **Dual submission:** previously published relevant work welcome for presentation.
- **Tracks:** technical; position paper.
- **Author reviewing obligation:** recruiting reviewers for both tracks; each reviewer receives at most 3 papers.
- **Portal:** OpenReview. Notification Sep 29 2026.
- **Negative results / replication:** scope names reproducibility checking and research evaluation; no explicit negative-results statement.

### Meta-Agents (Managing Agents that Manage Agents)
- **Scope:** responsible use of meta-agents; governance, oversight, societal impact.
- **Page limits / exclusions:** full ≤9 pages main text; short ≤4 pages. References and appendices excluded, but main text must be self-contained.
- **Template / footnote:** NeurIPS 2026 workshop LaTeX template. Footnote change NOT STATED.
- **Checklist:** a missing statement is explicit grounds for desk rejection.
- **Anonymity:** double-blind — names, affiliations, acknowledgments removed; prior work cited in third person.
- **Archival:** non-archival.
- **Dual submission:** work under submission elsewhere accepted.
- **Tracks:** main; Demo Track.
- **Author reviewing obligation:** NOT STATED. Authors should maintain up-to-date OpenReview profiles.
- **Portal:** OpenReview, private during review. Notification by Sep 29 2026; workshop Dec 11–12 2026.
- **Negative results:** no blanket welcome, but "honest analyses" language present.

- **Template / footnote:** official NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX template **plus a required `\workshoptitle{}` field**.
- **Checklist:** NOT STATED
- **Anonymity:** explicitly double-blind.
- **Archival:** explicitly non-archival; in-person poster session for all accepted papers.
- **Dual submission:** NOT STATED
